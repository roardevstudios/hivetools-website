v0.0.1 — First Public Beta

Hive First Public Beta Launch

September 2026 · Initial Public Release

  • First Public Beta of Hive: The local-first desktop AI assistant that actually takes action on Windows 10 & 11.
  • Next-Gen Pro Workspace UI: Clean 56px pro navigation rail, date-grouped conversation streams, tactile keyboard-driven composer, and sliding artifact canvas panel.
  • Free 50,000 AI Tokens Daily: Free daily quota for all beta testers, resetting every 24 hours.
  • Live Cited Web Research: Real-time web browsing with clickable verified source citations (domain, title, and snippets).
  • Real PC Automation: Safe desktop control allowing Hive to launch software, click, and run shell tasks — always guarded by mandatory human Allow/Deny prompts and audit logging.
  • Buddy Companion & Notch: Screen companion that lives at your cursor or pops from the notch anywhere with Ctrl+Shift+J.
  • Voice Mode: Push-to-talk voice dictation and spoken audio responses.
  • Agent Swarm: Multi-agent concurrency (Scout, Hive, Pulse, and Sentry) working together on complex workflows.

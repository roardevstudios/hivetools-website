#Overview

A Windows desktop workspace with core chat and plan-specific tools.

How Hive is organized

Hive is planned as a Windows desktop workspace. Its plan catalog separates core chat from research, agent, and PC workflows. The website provides account management and a scripted demo; it does not run prompts or control your computer.

#Security

Keep account credentials private and review desktop actions before approval.

Account safety

Hive uses Supabase for sign-in and account storage. Your browser keeps the active session locally so you stay signed in across Hive pages; you can sign out from your profile at any time.

  • Use a unique password and do not share your sign-in session.
  • Sign out on shared or public computers after using your account.
  • Contact apis.new.world@gmail.com if you see unexpected account activity.

For password recovery, use Forgot password? on the sign-in page.

#Privacy

How Hive handles account, usage, and local app data.

Last updated: October 2026. Hive ("we", "Apis") makes the Hive desktop app and the hivetools.pro website.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email and account profile information when you sign up.
  • Release email: your email address and opt-in time when you check the release-update box on the website or in your profile.
  • Purchase data: Checkout is not open yet; no Hive purchases can currently be made through this site.
  • Usage data: token and voice-session counters, so your daily quota is enforced on our side rather than only on your machine.
  • Local data stays local: your chats, settings and transcripts live on your machine, not on our servers. They are not synced between your devices.

How we use it

To run your account, enforce quotas, provide product information, and improve Hive. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Sharing

We use Supabase for account hosting, authentication and database services, and Google when you choose Google sign-in. Release updates are sent through Google Gmail from apis.new.world@gmail.com. Model requests are sent to the provider selected in the app. Payment processing is not active while checkout is closed.

Your rights

Turn off release updates in your Hive profile, or email apis.new.world@gmail.com to unsubscribe. You can also ask for a copy, correction or deletion of your data at that address. Deleting your account removes your profile, quota and subscription rows; PayPal keeps its own transaction records.

Retention

We keep account and purchase records while your account exists and up to 3 years after, for fraud prevention and accounting. Anonymized usage stats may be kept longer.

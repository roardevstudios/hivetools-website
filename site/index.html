Your PC, your call

What we can and can’t promise.

Permission, data handling, and who builds Hive.

Permission

Every PC action asks for Allow or Deny. Destructive commands are blocked before they reach the screen. Actions are logged locally.

Where data lives

Chats stay on your device; this site does not run prompts. Account and usage counters are handled by Supabase. Model requests go to the provider selected in the app.

Good to know

Hive, clearly explained.

A few quick answers about release status, plans, and data.

Is Hive available to download?+

The public beta installer was removed. We’ll explain what changed when v0.0.1 is posted.

What does Hive Free include?+

The planned Free tier includes 25,000 chat tokens per day, 5 voice sessions per day, standard models, and core chat.

Are paid plans one-time purchases?+

No. Starter, Student, and Max are planned monthly subscriptions. Checkout is not open yet.

Where do chats and prompts go?+

Chats stay on your device. This website does not run prompts. Model requests are sent to the provider selected in the app.

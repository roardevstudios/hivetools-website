A practical guide

Put your AI crew to work.

A preview of planned Hive app workflows, with plan availability called out below.

Try the scripted homepage demoExplore the guide

These are planned workflows, not a downloadable build. Research and PC actions are Starter. Buddy, the notch, and HiveBox are Plus. Nothing here runs on this website.

01 / Research

Get oriented on a topic

Planned from Starter: ask Hive to compare options, gather current information, and return an answer with sources you can inspect. This does not run on this website.

“Compare these options and show me the sources.”

02 / Planning

Turn an idea into a plan

Shape an idea into practical next steps with Hive’s AI crew. Scout, Hive, and Pulse work together. Critic reviews the answer. Sentry watches tool calls.

“Make a two-week launch plan for a small beta.”

03 / Collaboration

Bring the crew into one chat

Planned agent hand-offs bring research, a second opinion, and a review into one conversation. Scout, Hive, and Pulse work together. Critic reviews the answer. Sentry watches tool calls.

“Ask Scout to find examples, then have Critic review the plan.”

04 / PC control

Ask for a desktop action

Planned from Starter. The app shows the action and waits for Allow. This page cannot do it.

“Open Notepad and draft a checklist for me.”

05 / Deep work

Keep project work together

Bring project context into your Hive conversations and keep your work focused.

“Review this project brief and list the open decisions.”

06 / Quick help

Get help without breaking focus

Buddy and the notch are planned for Plus. Voice on Free is a small daily allowance of 5 sessions, not this whole workflow.

“Summarize this and give me the next step.”

Swipe to see more

Get better results.

  1. 01

    Say what success looks like

    Share the outcome you want, not only the action you imagine taking.

  2. 02

    Add useful context

    Include constraints, audience, timeframe, and any material the task depends on.

  3. 03

    Review before acting

    Check the sources and plan. Approve desktop actions only when they look right.

You stay in control of your PC

Website examples are illustrative. Real desktop actions run in the Hive app and require your approval. Your chats stay on your device.

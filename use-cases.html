A practical guide

Put your AI crew
to work.

Hive brings research, planning, collaboration, and permission-based PC control into one Windows workspace. Start with a goal, then work with the crew to shape the result.

Try the interactive demoExplore the guide
01 / RESEARCH
⌕

Get oriented on a topic

Ask Hive to compare options, gather current information, and return a concise answer with sources you can inspect. Follow up to narrow the question or check a claim.

“Compare these options and show me the sources.”
02 / PLANNING
↗

Turn an idea into a plan

Start with a rough goal. Hive Sparks can help shape the prompt, while Scout and Critic can help build and review a practical sequence of next steps.

“Make a two-week launch plan for a small beta.”
03 / COLLABORATION
⌘

Bring the crew into one chat

Use group chats and agent hand-offs to get different kinds of help in the same conversation: research, a second opinion, or a review before you move on.

“@scout find examples, then ask @critic to review the plan.”
04 / PC CONTROL
⌁

Ask for a desktop action

Buddy can open apps, click, and type to help complete a task. Hive shows the proposed action and waits for your approval before it runs.

“Open Notepad and draft a checklist for me.”
05 / DEEP WORK
▧

Keep project work together

Attach a project folder through HiveBox, keep chats around the work, and use the Mission Board to save reusable prompts and track follow-ups on your device.

“Review this project brief and list the open decisions.”
06 / QUICK HELP
✦

Get help without breaking focus

Use the Buddy notch and voice input for a quick question while another app is open. Return to the main workspace whenever you need more room.

“Summarize this and give me the next step.”
01 · Say what success looks like

Share the outcome you want, not only the action you imagine taking.

02 · Add useful context

Include constraints, audience, timeframe, and any material the task depends on.

03 · Review before acting

Check the sources and plan. Approve desktop actions only when they look right.

✓
You stay in control of your PC

Website examples are illustrative. Real desktop actions run in the Hive app and require your approval. Your chats and Mission Board stay on your device.

What's included at launch

Every plan includes these core features from day one.

Cited Research

Ask anything and get answers with live, verified sources — domain, title, snippet, and links you can click through yourself.

Real PC Control

Hive can open apps, run safe shell commands, click, and type — always through the Allow/Deny safety prompt.

Buddy Notch (coming soon)

Ask Buddy anywhere with Ctrl+Shift+J — the notch pops on your desktop even when Hive is minimized.

Voice In & Out

Dictate commands and hear responses spoken from the Hive desktop app.

Agent Swarm

Scout, Hive, and Pulse work together on one message. Sentry guards tool calls for safety and accuracy.

HiveBox Cloud

Your own disk for Hive. Store files, run scripts, track concurrent jobs. Toggle the panel anytime.
